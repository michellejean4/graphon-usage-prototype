# Graphon usage and upgrade components

The pieces used in the usage and upgrade prototype. They are built on shadcn/ui patterns with Tailwind v4, so they slot into Graphon's existing app.

Use the toolbar at the top to switch between **light and dark** mode and between the three **usage** states (plenty left, getting close, out of queries).

## Colours

| Token | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| `aqua-strong` | Queries, and the upgrade button |
| `mint-strong` | Storage |
| `coral-strong` | Members |
| `destructive` | A limit that has been hit |

## Rules

- One aqua upgrade button per area. Everything else stays neutral.
- Meters turn red only at 100%. Members never turn red, a full team is normal.
- The heads up appears at 80% and can be dismissed. At 100% it stays until the reset or an upgrade.
